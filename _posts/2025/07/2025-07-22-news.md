### Introducing OSS Rebuild: Open Source, Rebuilt to Last

font-size: 11pt; vertical-align: baseline; white-space: pre;"><p dir="ltr" style="line-height: 1.38; margin-bottom: 0pt; margin-top: 0pt;"><span style="font-size: 11pt; font-weight: 700; vertical-align: baseline;">Build observability and verification tools</span><span style="font-size: 11pt; vertical-align: baseline;"> that security teams can integrate into their existing vulnerability management workflows.</span></p></li><li dir="ltr" style="font-family: Arial, sans-serif; font-size: 11pt; vertical-align: baseline; white-space: pre;"><p dir="ltr" style="line-height: 1.38; margin-bottom: 0pt; margin-top: 0pt;"><span style="font-size: 11pt; font-weight: 700; vertical-align: baseline;">Infrastructure definitions</span><span style="font-size: 11pt; vertical-align: baseline;"> to allow organizations to easily run their own instances of OSS Rebuild to rebuild, generate, sign, and distribute provenance.</span></p></li></ul><br /><h4 dir="ltr" style="line-height: 1.38; margin-bottom: 4pt; margin-top: 14pt;"><span face="Arial, sans-serif" style="color: #666666; font-size: 12pt; font-weight: 400; vertical-align: baseline;">Challenges</span></h4><p dir="ltr" style="line-height: 1.38; margin-bottom: 0pt; margin-top: 0pt;"><span face="Arial, sans-serif" style="font-size: 11pt; vertical-align: baseline;">Open source software has become the foundation of our digital world. From critical infrastructure to everyday applications, OSS components now account for </span><a href="https://static.carahsoft.com/concrete/files/1617/1597/8665/2024_Open_Source_Security_and_Risk_Analysis_Report_WRAPPED.pdf"><span face="Arial, sans-serif" style="color: #1155cc; font-size: 11pt; vertical-align: baseline;">77%</span></a><span face="Arial, sans-serif" style="font-size: 11pt; vertical-align: baseline;"> of modern applications. With an estimated value exceeding </span><a href="https://www.hbs.edu/ris/Publication%20Files/24-038_51f8444f-502c-4139-8bf2-56eb4b65c58a.pdf"><span face="Arial, sans-serif" style="color: #1155cc; font-size: 11pt; vertical-align: baseline;">$12 trillion</span></a><span face="Arial, sans-serif" style="font-size: 11pt; vertical-align: baseline;">, open source software has never been more integral to the global economy.</span></p><br /><p dir="ltr" style="line-height: 1.38; margin-bottom: 0pt; margin-top: 0pt;"><span face="Arial, sans-serif" style="font-size: 11pt; vertical-align: baseline;">Yet this very ubiquity makes open source an attractive target: Recent high-profile supply chain attacks have demonstrated sophisticated methods for compromising widely-used packages. Each incident erodes trust in open ecosystems, creating hesitation among both contributors and consumers.</span></p><br /><p dir="ltr" style="line-height: 1.38; margin-bottom: 0pt; margin-top: 0pt;"><span face="Arial, sans-serif" style="font-size: 11pt; vertical-align: baseline;">The security community has responded with initiatives like </span><a href="http://scorecard.dev/"><span face="Arial, sans-serif" style="color: #1155cc; font-size: 11pt; vertical-align: baseline;">OpenSSF Scorecard</span></a><span face="Arial, sans-serif" style="font-size: 11pt; vertical-align: baseline;">, </span><a href="https://blog.pypi.org/posts/2023-04-20-introducing-trusted-publishers/"><span face="Arial, sans-serif" style="color: #1155cc; font-size: 11pt; vertical-align: baseline;">pypi's Trusted Publishers</span></a><span face="Arial, sans-serif" style="font-size: 11pt; vertical-align: baseline;">, and </span><a href="https://github.blog/2023-04-19-introducing-npm-package-provenance/"><span face="Arial, sans-serif" style="color: #1155cc; font-size: 11pt; vertical-align: baseline;">npm's native SLSA support</span></a><span face="Arial, sans-serif" style="font-size: 11pt; vertical-align: baseline;">. However, there is no panacea: Each effort targets a certain aspect of the problem, often making tradeoffs like shifting work onto publishers and maintainers.</span></p><h4 dir="ltr" style="line-height: 1.38; margin-bottom: 4pt; margin-top: 14pt;"><span face="Arial, sans-serif" style="color: #666666; font-size: 12pt; font-weight: 400; vertical-align: baseline;">Our Aim</span></h4><p dir="ltr" style="line-height: 1.38; margin-bottom: 0pt; margin-top: 0pt;"><span face="Arial, sans-serif" style="font-size: 11pt; vertical-align: baseline;">Our aim with OSS Rebuild is to empower the security community to deeply understand and control their supply chains by making package consumption as transparent as using a source repository. Our rebuild platform unlocks this transparency by utilizing a declarative build process, build instrumentation, and network monitoring capabilities which, within the </span><a href="https://slsa.dev/spec/v1.0/levels#build-track"><span face="Arial, sans-serif" style="color: #1155cc; font-size: 11pt; vertical-align: baseline;">SLSA Build</span></a><span face="Arial, sans-serif" style="font-size: 11pt; vertical-align: baseline;"> framework, produces fine-grained, durable, trustworthy security metadata.</span></p><br /><p dir="ltr" style="line-height: 1.38; margin-bottom: 0pt; margin-top: 0pt;"><span face="Arial, sans-serif" style="font-size: 11pt; vertical-align: baseline;">Building on the hosted infrastructure model that we pioneered with </span><a href="https://github.com/google/oss-fuzz"><span face="Arial, sans-serif" style="color: #1155cc; font-size: 11pt; vertical-align: baseline;">OSS Fuzz</span></a><span face="Arial, sans-serif" style="font-size: 11pt; vertical-align: baseline;"> for memory issue detection, OSS Rebuild similarly seeks to use hosted resources to address security challenges in open source, this time aimed at securing the software supply chain.</span></p><br /><p dir="ltr" style="line-height: 1.38; margin-bottom: 0pt; margin-top: 0pt;"><span face="Arial, sans-serif" style="font-size: 11pt; vertical-align: baseline;">Our vision extends beyond any single ecosystem: We are committed to bringing supply chain transparency and security to all open source software development. Our initial support for the PyPI (Python), npm (JS/TS), and Crates.io (Rust) package registries—providing rebuild provenance for many of their most popular packages—is just the beginning of our journey.</span></p><br /><h4 dir="ltr" style="line-height: 1.38; margin-bottom: 4pt; margin-top: 14pt;"><span face="Arial, sans-serif" style="color: #666666; font-size: 12pt; font-weight: 400; vertical-align: baseline;">How OSS Rebuild Works</span></h4><div class="separator" style="clear: both; text-align: center;"><a href="https://blogger.googleusercontent.com/img/a/AVvXsEjfsIwZQ4rw9fIh98NeN_LIDA02i6bu13nW4MHLQtGXCLKxdCQU3IMNCoy2eYlVrnTE3ntDMAwVgplosBHL-_ElPhAQNh1kBN3Hgz6QPq0mFcSIPlVC_pUqrsnF9_s6nNRg2j6DIfrDqLGt33Futda6HmSletctGX72E7d4_s-TQ7g_dNvZPtKIboF9esHb" style="margin-left: 1em; margin-right: 1em;"><img alt="" height="274" src="https://blogger.googleusercontent.com/img/a/AVvXsEjfsIwZQ4rw9fIh98NeN_LIDA02i6bu13nW4MHLQtGXCLKxdCQU3IMNCoy2eYlVrnTE3ntDMAwVgplosBHL-_ElPhAQNh1kBN3Hgz6QPq0mFcSIPlVC_pUqrsnF9_s6nNRg2j6DIfrDqLGt33Futda6HmSletctGX72E7d4_s-TQ7g_dNvZPtKIboF9esHb=w641-h274" width="641" /></a></div><br /><br /><p dir="ltr" style="line-height: 1.38; margin-bottom: 0pt; margin-top: 0pt;"><span face="Arial, sans-serif" style="font-size: 11pt; vertical-align: baseline;"></span></p><br /><p dir="ltr" style="line-height: 1.38; margin-bottom: 0pt; margin-top: 0pt;"><span face="Arial, sans-serif" style="font-size: 11pt; vertical-align: baseline;">Through automation and heuristics, we determine a prospective build definition for a target package and rebuild it. We semantically compare the result with the existing upstream artifact, normalizing each one to remove instabilities that cause bit-for-bit comparisons to fail (e.g. archive compression). Once we reproduce the package, we publish the build definition and outcome via </span><a href="https://slsa.dev/spec/v0.1/provenance"><span face="Arial, sans-serif" style="color: #1155cc; font-size: 11pt; vertical-align: baseline;">SLSA Provenance</span></a><span face="Arial, sans-serif" style="font-size: 11pt; vertical-align: baseline;">. This attestation allows consumers to reliably verify a package's origin within the source history, understand and repeat its build process, and customize the build from a known-functional baseline (or maybe even use it to generate more detailed </span><a href="https://slsa.dev/spec/v1.0/faq#q-how-does-slsa-and-slsa-provenance-relate-to-sbom"><span face="Arial, sans-serif" style="color: #1155cc; font-size: 11pt; vertical-align: baseline;">SBOMs</span></a><span face="Arial, sans-serif" style="font-size: 11pt; vertical-align: baseline;">).</span></p><br /><p dir="ltr" style="line-height: 1.38; margin-bottom: 0pt; margin-top: 0pt;"><span face="Arial, sans-serif" style="font-size: 11pt; vertical-align: baseline;">With OSS Rebuild's existing automation for PyPI, npm, and Crates.io, most packages obtain protection effortlessly without user or maintainer intervention. Where automation isn't currently able to fully reproduce the package, we offer manual build specification so the whole community benefits from individual contributions.</span></p><br /><p dir="ltr" style="line-height: 1.38; margin-bottom: 0pt; margin-top: 0pt;"><span face="Arial, sans-serif" style="font-size: 11pt; vertical-align: baseline;">And we are also excited at the potential for AI to help reproduce packages: Build and release processes are often described in natural language documentation which, while difficult to utilize with discrete logic, is increasingly useful to language models. Our initial experiments have demonstrated the approach's viability in automating exploration and testing, with limited human intervention, even in the most complex builds.</span></p><br /><h4 dir="ltr" style="line-height: 1.38; margin-top: 0px; padding-inline-start: 48px;"><li dir="ltr" style="font-family: Arial, sans-serif; font-size: 11pt; vertical-align: baseline; white-space: pre;"><p dir="ltr" style="line-height: 1.38; font-size: 11pt; vertical-align: baseline;">solana/webjs (2024)</span></a></p></li></ul><li dir="ltr" style="font-family: Arial, sans-serif; font-size: 11pt; vertical-align: baseline; white-space: pre;"><p dir="ltr" style="line-height: 1.38; margin-top: 0px; padding-inline-start: 48px;"><li dir="ltr" style="font-family: Arial, sans-serif; font-size: 11pt; vertical-align: baseline; white-space: pre;"><p dir="ltr" style="line-height: 1.38;
