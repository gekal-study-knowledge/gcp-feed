<li>Semaphore waits</li>
<li>Transaction lock waits</li>
</ul>

### How Target is enhancing retail discovery and cutting database maintenance by 50% with Spanner Graph

- **Link**: [https://cloud.google.com/blog/topics/retail/how-target-rebuilt-retail-discovery-with-spanner-graph/](https://cloud.google.com/blog/topics/retail/how-target-rebuilt-retail-discovery-with-spanner-graph/)
- **Published**: 2026-08-05 01:00:00
- **Fetched**: 2026-08-27 21:31:31

<div class="block-paragraph_advanced"><p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">In today’s retail environment, shoppers expect highly personalized product discovery experiences and conversational assistance that feels genuine, natural, and genuinely helpful. Today, successful product discovery is about understanding semantic meaning and the rich, connected relationships between products, categories, and guest intent. It is no longer just about keywords and basic browsing. </span></p>
<p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">At Target, this work is handled by our Guest Product Confidence platform team. They are responsible for building the features that establish trust and guide purchasing decisions, such as ratings, reviews, and AI-driven digital shopping assistants. An exciting example of this is our</span> <a href="https://www.target.com/gift-finder" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><span style="text-decoration: underline; vertical-align: baseline;">Gift Finder chat agent</span></a><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">, which we launched during the 2025 holiday season online and in the Target app to help shoppers discover the perfect items through friendly, conversational dialogue.</span></p>
<p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">To deliver real-time personalization and context-rich semantic responses like these at global scale, we identified a critical architectural need to move away from a fragmented data ecosystem toward a unified data platform. We needed a solution capable of supporting high-throughput transactional workloads, highly connected graph relationships, vector similarity search, and full-text keyword search all at once. </span></p>
<p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">In this post, we’ll explore how we achieved all four with Spanner.</span></p>
<h3><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">Overcoming fragmented architecture</span></h3>
<p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">Previously, Target’s discovery data ecosystem relied on a combination of Elasticsearch clusters for search and inverted indexes, alongside separate NoSQL datastores for our transactional data. While functional, this fragmented architecture presented significant operational and technical challenges.</span></p>
<ul>
<li style="vertical-align: baseline;">
<p><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">Disconnected context: </strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">Keeping separate search, vector, and transactional databases in perfect sync was a constant challenge. Siloed information led to missing context, disconnected attribute relationships, and inconsistent query results.</span></p>
</li>
<li style="vertical-align: baseline;">
<p><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">High operational overhead: </strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">Managing independent clusters, tuning search indexes, and handling complex, custom synchronization and aggregation logic required intensive manual intervention from our engineering teams.</span></p>
</li>
<li style="vertical-align: baseline;">
<p><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">Expansion bottlenecks:</strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;"> Expanding our retail data domains required adding new database collections, maintaining complex joins, and navigating weak transactional guarantees across our discovery and core transactional systems.</span></p>
</li>
<li style="vertical-align: baseline;">
<p><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">Siloed intelligence:</strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;"> We lacked the ability to query graph relationships, vector similarity, and keyword search indexes in a single transaction.</span></p>
</li>
</ul>
<p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">To build the next generation of AI-driven guest experiences, we needed to consolidate on one platform.</span></p>
<h3><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">Building the enterprise ontology on Spanner Graph</span></h3>
<p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">We evaluated multiple specialized technologies, including standalone vector databases and niche graph databases. However, adding more single-purpose databases would have only worsened our operational complexity and data synchronization pipelines.</span></p>
<p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">We ultimately chose</span> <a href="https://docs.cloud.google.com/spanner/docs/graph/overview"><span style="text-decoration: underline; vertical-align: baseline;">Spanner Graph</span></a><span style="vertical-align: baseline;"> to build our enterprise ontology, which is a "graph-of-graphs" paradigm that allows us to construct a massive, generative AI-powered shopping graph.</span></p>
<p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">By unifying our data, we bring semantic data, graph relationships, vector embeddings, and operational transactions under one roof. This establishes Spanner as our single authoritative source of truth for both transactional state and semantic intelligence.</span></p>
<p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">Our high-level architecture now consists of three core pillars:</span></p>
<p><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">1. Enterprise augmentation<br /></strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">This layer captures our enterprise retail catalog, aggregates relevant metadata from multiple backend sources, and utilizes generative AI for agentic data enrichment to dramatically improve the quality and depth of the product data we ingest.</span></p>
<p><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">2. Unified graph, vector, and search store</strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;"> <br /></span><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">Instead of shifting data across multiple databases, Spanner Graph stores our entity nodes, relationship edges, and vector embeddings in the same database engine. Spanner Graph natively supports multi-hop graph traversals, semantic vector similarity, and full-text keyword queries over our relational tables. Because this multi-model synergy is native, we get strict ACID transactions for absolute correctness across distributed workloads without the need for fragile external sync pipelines.</span></p>
<p><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">3. Orchestration and AI layer<br /></strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">This layer powers our conversational guest interfaces, utilizing rich, structured context fed directly from Spanner Graph to ground our LLMs. It extracts highly specific product relationships to power tools like the </span><a href="https://www.target.com/gift-finder" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><span style="text-decoration: underline; vertical-align: baseline;">Gift Finder</span></a><span style="vertical-align: baseline;"> while governing responsible AI processes and evaluating generated outputs.</span></p>
<h3><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">A smooth, zero-downtime incremental migration</span></h3>
<p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">Transitioning critical search and discovery infrastructure that millions of guests rely on required a cautious, zero-downtime approach. We executed this migration in four structured phases.</span></p>
<ol>
<li style="vertical-align: baseline;">
<p><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">Schema and ontology mapping:</strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;"> We defined the specific retail entities, such as products, categories, brands, and guest preferences, and their corresponding relationships within the Spanner Graph schema.</span></p>
</li>
<li style="vertical-align: baseline;">
<p><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">Data integration and parallel replay:</strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;"> We built mutation-based data integrations in a parallel pipeline. This allowed us to continuously replay live transactional updates, apply schema transformations, generate embeddings, and write them directly into Spanner Graph in real-time.</span></p>
</li>
<li style="vertical-align: baseline;">
<p><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">Canary deployment:</strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;"> We gradually shifted live read traffic to the new Spanner Graph-backed platform, validating query performance, semantic accuracy, and database stability under real retail workloads.</span></p>
</li>
<li style="vertical-align: baseline;">
<p><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">Cutover and cleanup:</strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;"> Once performance was thoroughly verified, we fully transitioned all search and discovery traffic to Spanner and deprecated our legacy Elasticsearch stack, entirely removing the maintenance burden of those clusters.</span></p>
</li>
</ol>
<h3><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">Business impact</span></h3>
<p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">By building directly on Spanner Graph, we unlocked measurable technical and business outcomes:</span></p>
<p><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">The ultimate GraphRAG foundation:</strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;"> Traditional RAG relies on flat vector similarity, which often misses the structured associations between products, such as matching a toy with its compatible accessories or age-appropriateness. By combining deep graph traversals with semantic vector search in a unified GraphRAG architecture, we grounded our LLMs with highly precise context. This directly improved our recommendation relevancy, enhanced guest satisfaction, and boosted our Net Promoter Score.</span></p>
<p><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">Consolidated SQL + GQL interoperability:</strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;"> With Spanner Graph, our developers query structured relational catalog data and connected graph relationships in a single query using standard SQL and GQL (Graph Query Language). This eliminates the need for data duplication, latency, or complex ETL pipelines to bridge these paradigms.</span></p>
<p><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">Serverless scalability with zero growth ceiling:</strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;"> Spanner automatically handled massive, unpredictable traffic spikes during peak retail events like Black Friday and Cyber Monday. Spanner's built-in autoscaler dynamically adjusted computing capacity to handle burst traffic during high-intensity, limited-time promotional offers without sacrificing performance.</span></p>
<p><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">50% reduction in infrastructure maintenance: </strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">By consolidating our transactional NoSQL and search index databases into a single managed Google Cloud service, we eliminated the operational burden of maintaining separate database clusters. Our developers now spend 50% less time on database administration and infrastructure upkeep, allowing us to build and deploy new, customer-facing AI features much faster.</span></p>
<p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">Migrating to Spanner Graph has accelerated our generative AI roadmap, serving as the ultimate proof of what is possible when you build on</span> <a href="https://cloud.google.com/transform/shift-system-of-action-architecting-the-agentic-data-cloud-AI"><span style="text-decoration: underline; vertical-align: baseline;">the right data foundation</span></a><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">. </span></p>

<div class="block-paragraph_advanced"><p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">When people think about digital transformation in banking, they often focus on the visible results: mobile apps and new digital services. But there's an invisible infrastructure making all these services possible: APIs. At </span><a href="https://www.db.com/" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><span style="text-decoration: underline; vertical-align: baseline;">Deutsche Bank</span></a><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">, we recognized that APIs aren't just technical plumbing; they're the nervous system of modern banking. </span></p>
<p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">A few years ago, our application landscape was dominated by monolithic systems. As we evaluated how to break them into modular, reusable APIs, one thing became clear: we couldn't just decompose our work into APIs — we needed a central API management platform (APIM) to manage what would emerge. We needed something where documentation, security policies, and governance all had to be built in from the start, not bolted on later. </span></p>
<p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">The question wasn't just how to modernize, but how to best serve our customers and position ourselves for tomorrow's opportunities, especially with emerging technological paradigm shifts. </span></p>
<p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">Needing a system that was adaptable, scalable, reliable, secure, and AI-ready for the demands of modern banking, we chose </span><a href="https://cloud.google.com/apigee"><span style="text-decoration: underline; vertical-align: baseline;">Google Cloud's Apigee</span></a><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;"> </strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">as our APIM platform. </span></p>
<h3><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">Building the backbone: four key capabilities </strong></h3>
<p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">Today, Apigee manages our API ecosystem — from open banking APIs connecting us with fintech partners, to the internal microservices powering our various banking platforms, and even the client-facing applications that enable seamless digital experiences such as online banking. </span></p>
<p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">Here are four important capabilities the platform offers us:</span></p>
<p><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">1. Unified governance without sacrificing speed </strong></p>
<p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">Apigee is the foundation of our API catalog. Every endpoint, version, and dependency is documented and discoverable. Development teams find and reuse existing APIs rather than rebuild functionality. We've moved from "Where's that customer data API?" — which took days — to a searchable, real-time catalog accessible to any developer. </span></p>
<p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">But governance isn't about bottlenecks, it's about guardrails, and with Apigee's policy framework, we automatically enforce standards. OpenAPI specifications, schema validation, and error handling are now baked into the platform. Teams move faster </span><span style="font-style: italic; vertical-align: baseline;">because </span><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">they work within consistent frameworks. </span></p>
<p><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">2. Security: the employee onboarding analogy </strong></p>
<p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">When thinking about API security, imagine onboarding a new employee. You don't give them access to every system on day one. You follow the least privilege principle, so they get exactly the permissions needed for their role. If they switch departments, their access rights will be updated. If they leave the company, access is revoked immediately. Apigee works the same way for our services and applications. </span></p>
<p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">When connecting a new service — say, one that accesses customer accounts — we don't open the floodgates. Through OAuth2 scopes and API key management, we define precisely what that agent can access: </span></p>
<ul>
<li><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">Read account balances? Yes. </span></li>
<li><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">Initiate wire transfers? No. </span></li>
<li><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">Access 90-day transaction history? Yes. </span></li>
<li><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">Full historical data? Only with elevated permissions. </span></li>
</ul>
<p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">Like employee access, these permissions are centrally managed, regularly audited, and instantly revocable. Just as we track employee activity for compliance, Apigee logs every API call to see who accessed what data, when, and why. </span></p>
<p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">This becomes critical with high-volume automated systems. An automated service doesn't take breaks and can make thousands of calls per minute if misconfigured. Rate limiting and quota enforcement ensure that even when something goes wrong, the blast radius is contained. </span></p>
<p><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">3. Resilience and performance at scale </strong></p>
<p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">Banking doesn't have downtime. When customers check balances at 3 a.m. or markets surge with trading activity, our APIs must respond instantly and reliably. </span></p>
<p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">Apigee's load balancing and auto-scaling evenly distribute that traffic. Health checks and circuit breakers automatically route around struggling services, and for frequently accessed data, Apigee's caching delivers sub-millisecond responses without hitting backends. </span></p>
<p><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">4. Observability: measuring everything </strong></p>
<p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">Before Apigee, understanding API performance was like assembling a jigsaw puzzle with pieces from different boxes. Now we have unified dashboards showing real-time traffic, error rates by service, usage analytics by consumer, and compliance metrics. This visibility serves operations, product managers who track partner value, and security teams who identify anomalies.</span></p></div>

<div class="block-paragraph_advanced"><h3><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">The path forward </strong></h3>
<p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">We built this infrastructure for the API economy, and in doing so, we have also built a strong foundation for the future of digital banking. As the industry evolves, this API-first approach will be critical for integrating next-generation services. </span></p>
<p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">As digital banking continues to advance, a shift toward intelligent services that can react, predict, and assist in real time is underway. Capabilities such as real</span><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">-</span><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">time pattern recognition, predictive insights, and AI</span><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">-</span><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">powered assistants are becoming part of everyday digital experiences, with their visibility and impact increasing as adoption accelerates. Each of these capabilities will consume APIs — and they will introduce new requirements: ultra</span><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">-</span><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">low latency, high</span><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">-</span><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">throughput data flows, and secure orchestration across multiple APIs. </span></p>
<p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">Because we invested in a flexible API platform with Apigee, we are well-positioned to adapt and optimize our infrastructure for these future needs, rather than having to rebuild it. </span></p>
<h3><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">Emerging standards: MCP, A2A, and the future </strong></h3>
<p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">The industry is exploring new integration standards. Protocols like </span><a href="https://modelcontextprotocol.io/docs/getting-started/intro" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><span style="text-decoration: underline; vertical-align: baseline;">Model Context Protocol (MCP)</span></a><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;"> </strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">and Google's </span><a href="https://developers.googleblog.com/en/a2a-a-new-era-of-agent-interoperability/" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><span style="text-decoration: underline; vertical-align: baseline;">Agent2Agent (A2A)</span></a><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;"> </strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">are interesting because they build on existing API infrastructure. </span></p>
<p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">Our Apigee-managed APIs are well-positioned to leverage these advancements. For instance, MCP could benefit from our OpenAPI specifications, and A2A could leverage our OAuth2 framework, with both relying on the governance we've built. </span></p>
<p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">We're also exploring patterns like placing new types of servers behind Apigee proxies to maintain security controls while enabling modern workflows. Our "always-API" pattern ensures that services benefit from centralized management, no matter how they are accessed.</span></p></div>

<div class="block-paragraph_advanced"><h3><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">The vision: APIs as universal interface </strong></h3>
<p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">Every banking capability will eventually be exposed as an API. That’s not because APIs are trendy, but because they're the most flexible, composable, and governable way to share functionality, whether consumed by mobile apps, partner fintechs, analytics platforms, or other automated agents. </span></p>
<p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">At Deutsche Bank, this shift is already taking shape. The same API foundation that powers our core platforms is now enabling our evolution toward more intelligent, AI</span><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">-</span><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">supported services across the bank. That foundation provides the consistency, governance, and scalability needed to bring these capabilities to life, ensuring that as new intelligent services emerge, they can be integrated seamlessly, securely, and at enterprise scale. </span></p>
<p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">Apigee makes this possible by providing governance that scales across all use cases. It's not about controlling innovation; it's about enabling it safely. </span></p>
