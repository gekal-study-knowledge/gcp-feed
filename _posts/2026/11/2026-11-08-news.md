<h3>Feature</h3>
<p><strong>Case activity tagging</strong></p>
<p>You can now use custom tags for individual activities, comments, tasks, and
actions on the Case Wall, and filter the wall view by those tags. This allows
you to categorize specific observations and filter the timeline to view only the
entries relevant to your investigation or team.</p>
<p>For more information, see <a href="https://docs.cloud.google.com/chronicle/docs/soar/investigate/working-with-cases/define-tags-in-cases-admin#add-activity-tag">Add a tag to a Case Wall activity</a>.</p>
