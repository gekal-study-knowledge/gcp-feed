<h3><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">1. Gemini delivers one universal agent across the enterprise</strong></h3>
<p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">In October, we announced a unified Gemini experience. The same Gemini you collaborate with every day is now your enterprise agent to get real work done. Instead of navigating disconnected developer consoles, consumer tools, and workplace add-ons, Gemini meets work wherever it already happens – collaborating natively inside surfaces like Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, internal chats, web, and mobile.</span></p>
<p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">You give Gemini high-level business objectives rather than step-by-step instructions, and it orchestrates the necessary tools, context, and coworker agents to deliver a finished result. As employees engage it, the agent retains persistent memory, understands your specific business language, and respects enterprise permissions across every device. When a team automates a complex procedure, they can package it into a shared skill, turning one group’s procedural knowledge into a capability anyone across the organization can use.</span></p>
<p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">That shared foundation is what transforms agent building from an isolated engineering backlog into an organization-wide capability. At </span><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">Dojo</strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">, one of UK’s leading payment providers, non-technical teams used this exact environment to stand up over 600 custom agents in just two months:</span></p>
<p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">“When you can use natural language, anyone from HR to sales can turn their unique business experience into a custom AI agent in minutes. In two months, we created over 600 agents. The majority were built by our frontline business teams right inside Gemini, leaving our developers to focus on more complex challenges.” – Robert Howes, Chief Technology Officer, Dojo.</span></p></div>

<div class="block-paragraph_advanced"><h3><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">2. We bring the leading frontier models under one roof</strong></h3>
<p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">Google Cloud is now the only hyperscale cloud provider offering access to all three leading frontier model families, from Google, Anthropic and OpenAI, on a unified enterprise platform – and across every surface, from developer tooling in Antigravity to everyday productivity in Gemini for web, mobile, and desktop. Through the model garden, organizations can access over 200 models, drawing down existing cloud commitments on a single invoice without having to negotiate separate contracts.</span></p>
<p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">At the same time, we are deeply proud of the pace of innovation coming out of Google DeepMind. Over the past month alone, we’ve rolled out a full generation of specialized models: </span><a href="https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-4-argon/" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><strong style="text-decoration: underline; vertical-align: baseline;">Gemini 4 Argon</strong></a><span style="vertical-align: baseline;"> for strong performance across complex software engineering, law, finance, and cyber defense; </span><a href="https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/introducing-gemini-4-carbon/" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><strong style="text-decoration: underline; vertical-align: baseline;">Gemini 4 Carbon</strong></a><span style="vertical-align: baseline;"> for frontier performance in cybersecurity capabilities and long-horizon agentic workflows; and now </span><a href="https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/introducing-gemini-4-hydrogen/" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><strong style="text-decoration: underline; vertical-align: baseline;">Gemini 4 Hydrogen</strong></a><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">, bringing cutting-edge intelligence with speed and efficiency to everyday coding and agentic execution.</span></p>
<p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">By treating models as a diversified portfolio, we give our customers the freedom to optimize for speed, precision, and cost across every task. For </span><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">PayPal</strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">, this flexibility is central to their strategy:</span></p>
<p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">“We built a unified LLM stack. That meant we ran our open source models on top of our inferencing stack on GKE, and we ran our close-sourced models on top of the Gemini platform. Developers get choice – Claude, Gemini, Open Source – through one consistent governed access layer. That’s why we love the model garden.” — Srinivasan Manoharan, Director of AI/ML Platform, PayPal.</span></p></div>

<div class="block-paragraph_advanced"><h3><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">3. Optimizing spend at every layer of the stack</strong></h3>
<p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">A recurring concern we hear from enterprise leaders is the unpredictability of AI billing. When teams start scaling agents, pay-as-you-go pricing can feel like an open tab with no ceiling. That is why we optimize cost across every layer of the stack – co-designing custom TPU silicon, cluster networking, and foundation models as a single system to drive down baseline costs.</span></p>
<p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">At the execution level, we give finance and engineering teams the right levers to innovate without risking their margins. Gemini dynamically matches every task to the most cost-effective model, handling high-volume tasks with efficient models, only escalating to frontier reasoning models for complex problem-solving. Paired with native FinOps controls – including project-level monthly spend caps, anomaly detection, and Flexible Savings Plans – leaders get hard financial boundaries and clear visibility.</span></p>
<p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">For companies processing massive volumes of data, this approach makes all the difference. At </span><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">Imgix</strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">, the engineering team prioritized this architectural discipline as they scaled visual processing:</span></p>
<p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">“If we're using the most expensive model every single time, that's going to be a big bill for our customers. So we use a cheaper model like Gemini Flash to figure out, ‘Does this image or video even need this AI feature?’ before routing further down the pipeline.” – Alfonso Acosta, Head of Engineering, Imgix.</span></p></div>

<div class="block-paragraph_advanced"><h3><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">4. A single control plane for governed enterprise context</strong></h3>
<p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">When organizations empower employees to build and deploy agents at scale, governance cannot be an afterthought. It must be an inherent part of the environment in which those agents operate. With Gemini, every agent is assigned a dedicated Agent Identity, ensuring that all database queries, tool actions, and state transitions generate an auditable record tied directly to authorized roles. Execution occurs inside isolated runtime sandboxes, where Agent Gateway and Model Armor automatically scrub sensitive data, block policy violations, and enforce corporate compliance in real time.</span></p>
<p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">That same control plane extends to how agents interact with corporate intelligence. Rather than requiring organizations to move, replicate, or duplicate sensitive records into yet another external repository, Gemini connects directly to live enterprise systems. Agents ground their reasoning in data across BigQuery, Cloud Storage, SAP, Salesforce, S3, and Azure – inheriting existing user access controls and keeping proprietary data strictly within its original perimeter. </span></p>
<p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">Governed context and centralized control are what make company-wide agent adoption possible. </span><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">UKG</strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;"> relied on Gemini’s security, governance, and evaluation controls to skip building that foundation from scratch:</span></p>
<p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">“The Gemini platform, it’s solving some of these key problems that we already were trying to solve — from hosting agents to securing them to the governance around them, evals to make sure they’re behaving correctly. I can just use these things out of the box and get back to creating value.” – Thomas Newton, Head of Cloud and Engineering, UKG.</span></p></div>
