## Google Cloud Release Notes

<div class="entry-summary">
<h2 class="release-note-product-title">NetApp Volumes</h2>
<h3>Announcement</h3>
<p>Through December 31, 2026, Google Cloud NetApp Volumes sends email notifications
to the Google group for NetApp Volumes maintenance, in addition to
<a href="https://docs.cloud.google.com/unified-maintenance/docs/overview">Unified Maintenance</a> alerts.
After December 31, 2026, NetApp Volumes no longer sends email notifications to
the Google group, and Unified Maintenance is the only source of planned
maintenance notifications.</p>
<p>For more information, see
<a href="https://docs.cloud.google.com/netapp/volumes/docs/monitor/maintenance-events">Monitor and plan for maintenance events</a>.</p>
<h3>Feature</h3>
<p>Google Cloud NetApp Volumes is integrated with
<a href="https://docs.cloud.google.com/unified-maintenance/docs/overview">Unified Maintenance</a>. This integration
supports the Flex Unified, Flex File, Standard, Premium, and Extreme service
levels.</p>
<p>You can view upcoming and past planned maintenance events for your storage pools
in Cloud Hub, and track event logs and configure automated alerts in Cloud
Logging. For more information, see
<a href="https://docs.cloud.google.com/netapp/volumes/docs/monitor/maintenance-events">Monitor and plan for maintenance events</a>.</p>
