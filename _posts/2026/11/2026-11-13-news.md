### Google named the furthest in vision for the Gartner Magic Quadrant for Data Lakehouse Platforms

- **Link**: [https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/data-analytics/google-is-a-leader-in-the-gartner-magic-quadrant-for-data-lakehouse-platforms/](https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/data-analytics/google-is-a-leader-in-the-gartner-magic-quadrant-for-data-lakehouse-platforms/)

<div class="block-paragraph_advanced"><p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">Gartner has named Google a Leader, positioned furthest in vision, in its first-ever Magic Quadrant for Data  Lakehouse Platforms. In our opinion, this recognition validates a fundamental shift our customers are experiencing every day: In the agentic era, the enterprise lakehouse is no longer a passive data repository,  but a foundation where agents reason and act across all your data.</span></p>
<p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">The lakehouse originally emerged to bring data warehouse reliability to open, low-cost storage, but the benchmark defining the category is now shifting. Leading enterprises are moving rapidly from static reporting to autonomous AI agents that monitor operations, flag anomalies, and execute business workflows in real time. To support that shift, organizations need a platform where thousands of agents can read, reason, and act continuously across enterprise data without having to move that data first. Traditional, fragmented data stacks burdened by brittle pipelines, duplicated storage, and disjointed governance simply were not designed for the scale, performance, and latency required for agentic-scale workloads.</span></p>
<p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">We engineered our borderless Lakehouse at every layer of the stack to eliminate this complexity and hidden cost. Specifically designed for agents to reason across petabyte-scale data, the borderless Lakehouse unifies your data estate across clouds, data platforms, and SaaS applications so you can ground agents in governed business context and bring Gemini directly to your data. This allows your teams to move from managing infrastructure to orchestrating business outcomes.</span></p></div>

<div class="block-paragraph_advanced"><h3><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">Activate your global data footprint in place</span></h3>
<p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">For most organizations, the biggest barrier to scaling AI is that enterprise data lives everywhere, spanning multiple clouds, operational databases, and SaaS platforms. Moving all of that data into a single physical location is slow, expensive, and introduces governance risk.</span></p>
<p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">Our </span><a href="https://cloud.google.com/products/lakehouse"><strong style="text-decoration: underline; vertical-align: baseline;">borderless Lakehouse</strong></a><span style="vertical-align: baseline;"> </span><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">acts as the bridge across your distributed environment to activate your data. Built on open </span><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">Apache Iceberg</strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">, it connects your multi-cloud, SaaS, and operational data into a single governed fabric. Organizations can start analyzing datasets across AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud  immediately without costly data copies or complex migrations, benefitting from:</span></p>
<ul>
<li><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">Openness without performance trade-offs:</strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;"> Run analytics and AI across BigQuery and Managed Service for Apache Spark directly on open Iceberg tables with similar price-performance as native storage.</span></li>
<li><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">High-speed cross-cloud economics:</strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;"> Establishing private, high-speed paths to AWS and Azure is simple and cost-effective through </span><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">Cross-Cloud Interconnect and intelligent caching</strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">. By caching only the active data blocks your queries touch, the lakehouse serves repeated lookups locally, protecting data residency and eliminating variable egress fees.</span></li>
<li><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">Vertically integrated security and governance: </strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">Keeping data protected wherever it resides is non-negotiable. </span><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">Credential vending </strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">in</span><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;"> </strong><a href="https://docs.cloud.google.com/lakehouse/docs/about-lakehouse-catalogs"><strong style="text-decoration: underline; vertical-align: baseline;">Lakehouse runtime catalog</strong></a><span style="vertical-align: baseline;"> helps ensure security policies travel directly with your agents wherever your data resides.</span></li>
</ul>
<p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">German banking leader </span><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">Commerzbank</strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;"> built its AI strategy on our borderless Lakehouse and Apache Iceberg to deploy AI agents across contact centers and marketing while satisfying strict European banking regulations. A unified foundation now powers AI across the bank. The marketing team reduced campaign production times from weeks to days and empowered contact center agents with verified next steps in seconds.</span></p>
<h3><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">Agents need business context, not just access</span></h3>
<p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">An autonomous agent is only as accurate as the business context it understands. The lakehouse bridges the gap between agents and enterprise data, automatically harvesting metadata directly from your storage layers and passing it to your context engines. The </span><a href="https://docs.cloud.google.com/lakehouse/docs/about-lakehouse-catalogs"><span style="text-decoration: underline; vertical-align: baseline;">Lakehouse runtime catalog</span></a><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;"> </strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">integrates directly with </span><a href="https://cloud.google.com/products/knowledge-catalog"><span style="text-decoration: underline; vertical-align: baseline;">Knowledge Catalog</span></a><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">, which acts as your universal context engine for your enterprise data. It autonomously aggregates and curates metadata and semantic context from all your sources, within and outside Google, enriches them to extract meaning, and serves this unified context to all the agents that you build and use. Key Knowledge Catalog capabilities include:</span></p>
<ul>
<li><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">Unified aggregation across the enterprise:</strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;"> A single, governed semantic layer prevents metric drift across multi-cloud assets. With the general availability of </span><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">expanded aggregation</strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;"> in </span><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">Knowledge Catalog</strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">, organizations can unify metadata, metrics, and business logic across Looker, dbt, and BigQuery in one plane.</span></li>
<li><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">Turnkey agent grounding:</strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;"> Anchoring agents in verified business logic accelerates development and builds trust. </span><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">Gemini grounded with Knowledge Catalog</strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;"> and </span><a href="https://docs.cloud.google.com/knowledge-catalog/docs/context-sets"><strong style="text-decoration: underline; vertical-align: baseline;">context sets,</strong></a><span style="vertical-align: baseline;"> both in preview, let teams package curated metrics and instructions into token-efficient bundles that agents consume via standard APIs and Model Context Protocol.</span></li>
<li><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">No-code context optimization:</strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;"> Delivering only the most relevant context reduces latency, lowers token costs, and prevents expensive reasoning loops. With a </span><a href="https://docs.cloud.google.com/knowledge-catalog/docs/knowledge-studio"><span style="text-decoration: underline; vertical-align: baseline;">new studio experience in Knowledge Catalog</span></a><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">, in preview, data stewards get an agentic-first, no-code environment to resolve definition gaps and securely govern the exact context that agents are authorized to access.</span></li>
</ul>
<p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">Bloomberg Media grounded their conversational queries directly in the </span><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">Knowledge Catalog</strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;"> semantic layer, achieving a 63% improvement in query accuracy and eliminating hallucinations across their business teams.</span></p>
<p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">Business context is only half the equation. Because agents act rather than just analyze, your analytical lakehouse and operational databases have to behave as one system. That is why BigQuery, Managed Service for Apache Spark, Spanner, and AlloyDB are built to work together, making the borderless Lakehouse the data foundation for the entire Agentic Data Cloud.</span></p>

<p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">Unlocking the full potential of your lakehouse requires transforming how people work with data. Historically, data engineers, data scientists, and business users operated in disconnected silos. The lakehouse acts as the collaborative connective tissue by integrating </span><a href="http://google.com/url?q=https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/data-analytics/borderless-lakehouse-integrates-with-gemini&amp;sa=D&amp;source=docs&amp;ust=1792995621599108&amp;usg=AOvVaw3qqnlFgGVMNfCQDnTJS8FS"><strong style="text-decoration: underline; vertical-align: baseline;">directly with Gemini</strong></a><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">, allowing you to build, test, and operate AI agents directly on top of your unified lakehouse data.</span></p>
<ul>
<li><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">Intent-driven workflows for technical teams:</strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;"> With the </span><a href="https://cloud.google.com/blog/topics/developers-practitioners/data-agent-kit-is-now-ga-bring-google-data-cloud-to-any-coding-agent"><span style="text-decoration: underline; vertical-align: baseline;">Data Agent Kit</span></a><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">, data practitioners can write code, build notebooks, and run ML models directly from their preferred IDEs and CLIs using simple text prompts. Instead of writing boilerplate code from scratch, teams can use pre-built agentic skills and MCP tools to build complex data pipelines and work seamlessly across 15+ Google Cloud data services</span></li>
<li><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">Proactive and personalized insights for business users: </strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">Conversational analytics gives business users trusted answers in plain language, without waiting on data teams to build custom reports. </span><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">One-click publishing </strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">extends this, instantly turning approved technical models into conversational tools, allowing employees to ask plain-language questions, run accurate reports, and safely ground custom agents in daily workflows without any coding experience.</span></li>
</ul>
<p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">PayPal connected their Looker semantic models directly to AI agents using these capabilities, allowing business intelligence teams to define metric models once so that 5,000 employees could query live data with near 100% accuracy, accelerating insights by 50%.</span></p>
<h3><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">Bringing AI-powered engines to the Lakehouse</span></h3>
<p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">To make decisions that impact your business in real time, AI agents need to run complex reasoning loops directly where your data lives. We bring Google's leading artificial intelligence and high-performance processing engines directly into open table formats, enabling you to extract massive value without the complexity of duplicating data. Consider the following use cases:</span></p>

<h3><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">Maximize efficiency at scale without cost spirals</span></h3>

### Powering agentic cloud governance: What's new in Organization Policy

- **Link**: [https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/identity-security/ai-powered-cloud-governance-whats-new-in-organization-policy/](https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/identity-security/ai-powered-cloud-governance-whats-new-in-organization-policy/)
- **Published**: 2026-11-13 02:00:00
- **Fetched**: 2026-11-13 06:43:45

<details class="entry-summary">
<summary>詳細を表示</summary>
<div class="block-paragraph_advanced"><p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">Even as sophisticated, new zero-day exploits continue to emerge, most cybersecurity incidents still start from the same common sources: an attacker locating and exploiting a fundamental, easily preventable cloud misconfiguration.</span></p>
<p><a href="https://docs.cloud.google.com/organization-policy/overview"><span style="text-decoration: underline; vertical-align: baseline;">Organization Policy Service</span></a><span style="vertical-align: baseline;"> can help you secure resource configurations and set up security guardrails across your Google Cloud resource hierarchy. Using a catalog of over 100 predefined policies for many common cloud governance needs, as well as </span><a href="https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/identity-security/announcing-custom-org-policy-to-help-tailor-resource-guardrails-with-confidence?e=48754805"><span style="text-decoration: underline; vertical-align: baseline;">Custom Organization Policy</span></a><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">, you can more easily set up boundaries with granular control over resources.</span></p>
<p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">To help platform and security teams author, audit, and scale guardrails with greater speed and simplicity, we are introducing the general availability of our Organization Policy Model Context Protocol (MCP) server and natural-language constraint authoring powered by Gemini.</span></p>
<h3><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">Managing Organization Policy with agents</span></h3>
<p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">Rather than forcing administrators to manually parse complex hierarchy chains, the </span><a href="https://docs.cloud.google.com/organization-policy/reference/mcp"><span style="text-decoration: underline; vertical-align: baseline;">Organization Policy MCP server</span></a><span style="vertical-align: baseline;"> offers tools that allow autonomous agents and pipelines to interact directly with Organization Policy. </span></p>
<p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">Using the Organization Policy MCP server, agents can assist administrators with posture audits, drift detection, and exemption management. Key capabilities include: </span></p>
<ul>
<li style="vertical-align: baseline;">
<p><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">Constraint discovery</strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">: Tools like '</span><span style="font-style: italic; vertical-align: baseline;">ListConstraints</span><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">' and '</span><span style="font-style: italic; vertical-align: baseline;">GetCustomConstraint</span><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">' allow agents to understand the specific guardrails that apply to a project, folder, or organization.</span></p>
</li>
<li style="vertical-align: baseline;">
<p><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">Policy evaluation</strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">: The '</span><span style="font-style: italic; vertical-align: baseline;">GetEffectivePolicy</span><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">' tool calculates the effective policy after considering hierarchy inheritance and tag-based conditions. You can use this to verify if a configuration change will be compliant. </span></p>
</li>
<li style="vertical-align: baseline;">
<p><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">Manage policy</strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">: '</span><span style="font-style: italic; vertical-align: baseline;">CreatePolicy</span><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">' and '</span><span style="font-style: italic; vertical-align: baseline;">CreateCustomConstraint</span><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">' allow platform teams to manage guardrails via conversational workflows.</span></p>
</li>
</ul>
<p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">For example, you can combine your preferred coding agent with the Organization Policy MCP tools to create a custom dashboard to visualize the constraints applied to an organization and identify the folders and projects in the organization that have been exempted from the policy. You can also build custom agents and skills to streamline managing Organization Policy.</span></p></div>

<div class="block-paragraph_advanced"><h3><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">Simplifying custom constraint authoring with Gemini</span></h3>
<p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">While the </span><a href="https://docs.cloud.google.com/organization-policy/reference/org-policy-constraints#available_constraints"><span style="text-decoration: underline; vertical-align: baseline;">Organization Policy constraint catalog</span></a><span style="vertical-align: baseline;"> provides built-in managed constraints for common use cases, each organization has its own unique architectural standards. </span><a href="https://docs.cloud.google.com/organization-policy/overview#custom-constraints"><span style="text-decoration: underline; vertical-align: baseline;">Custom Organization Policy</span></a><span style="vertical-align: baseline;"> enables customers to express these standards as a custom constraint and enforce them across supported services. </span></p>

Today, administrators can author custom Organization Policy in natural language through a Gemini prompt. </span></p>
<p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">For example, you can prompt Gemini to require the use of agent-identity in </span><a href="https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-agent-platform/build/runtime?hl=ja"><span style="text-decoration: underline; vertical-align: baseline;">Agent Runtime</span></a><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">, to require that all agents deployed to Agent Runtime use an approved Agent Gateway, and to prevent users from disabling auto-upgrades on GKE, and refine custom constraints conversationally. </span></p>
<p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">To help ensure safe deployments, Gemini can also generate compliant and non-compliant test resource configurations that can help you test policy and validate in dry-run mode before enforcing the constraint.</span></p></div>

### Simplifying last mile cloud connectivity with Lumen and partner Cross-Cloud Interconnect

<details class="entry-summary">
<summary>詳細を表示</summary>
<div class="block-paragraph_advanced"><p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">Cross-Cloud Interconnect is expanding to connect to more of your workloads. We designed Google Cloud's </span><a href="https://cloud.google.com/solutions/cross-cloud-network"><span style="text-decoration: underline; vertical-align: baseline;">Cross-Cloud Network</span></a><span style="vertical-align: baseline;"> to simplify and optimize how organizations connect and secure applications across cloud and on-premises environments. Last year, we </span><a href="https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/networking/extending-cross-cloud-interconnect-to-aws-and-partners/"><span style="text-decoration: underline; vertical-align: baseline;">launched partner Cross-Cloud Interconnect for AWS</span></a><span style="vertical-align: baseline;"> based on an open specification, turning complex cross-cloud network builds into a managed service that provisions in minutes. </span></p>
<p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">Today, we are extending </span><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">partner Cross-Cloud Interconnect for last mile</strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">, starting in preview with Lumen Technologies, to bring that same simplicity and high-bandwidth connectivity to private clouds, on-premises data centers, neoclouds, and other clouds with expanded reach.</span></p>
