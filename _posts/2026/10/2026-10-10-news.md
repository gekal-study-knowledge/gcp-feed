<div class="block-paragraph_advanced"><p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">Alteryx One Live Query and Google Cloud BigQuery redefine how enterprises handle complex, unstructured data at scale by reducing reliance on disconnected tools, minimizing data movement, and enabling warehouse-native execution.</span></p>
<p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">Live Query provides an intuitive, browser-based environment that allows business users to build sophisticated data pipelines without writing a single line of code. When paired with the scale and performance of BigQuery, this duo enables secure SQL pushdown, meaning data transformation logic is executed directly within the warehouse rather than moving it across the network. This synergy is particularly potent for document-heavy workflows, where Live Query can orchestrate Google’s advanced AI and machine learning models — like Gemini — to extract intelligence from PDFs and images while maintaining the strict data governance and security of the BigQuery environment.</span></p>
<h3><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">From invoice PDFs to govern exception reporting in BigQuery with Live Query</strong></h3>
<p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">Finance and operations teams often find themselves buried under a mountain of vendor invoices. These documents come as PDFs with inconsistent data, varying invoice number formats, and a high risk of manual error. Traditionally, data teams have had to resort to manual data extraction or use a fragmented series of tools to move data into a warehouse — a process that is both time-consuming and prone to duplicates or amount mismatches.</span></p>
<p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">With the integration of </span><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">Live Query for BigQuery</strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">, you can now automate the entire lifecycle of an invoice. By processing PDFs, extracting key fields, and standardizing results directly within the BigQuery ecosystem, data teams can identify exceptions like anomalies or data issues within the documents faster while ensuring their governed enterprise data configured through the BigQuery Ffine grained access policies never has to leave the warehouse.</span></p>
<h3><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">Solution overview: Extract, classify, validate, route, and post</strong></h3>
<p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">Let’s dive deeper into how the Live Query solution works. Invoice PDFs arrive as unstructured documents in Google Cloud Storage. In the Live Query workflow, the Document Extract tool extracts structured fields using the model selected by the user, such as Gemini or Document AI. The results are then standardized for reliable matching and compared against historical invoice records already stored and governed in BigQuery. </span></p>
<p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">This automated workflow is designed to move from document to decision with minimal manual intervention. At enterprise scale, that pattern is not just about processing a handful of PDFs — it is about enabling analytics and AI over millions of unstructured documents while keeping reconciliation logic aligned with BigQuery. </span></p>
<p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">By combining AI-driven extraction with BigQuery-native execution, the solution provides:</span></p>
<ul>
<li style="vertical-align: baseline;">
<p><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">Higher straight-through processing</strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">: Reducing manual handling and allowing more invoices to move through the process automatically.</span></p>
</li>
<li style="vertical-align: baseline;">
<p><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">Faster reconciliation and fewer manual exceptions:</strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;"> Comparing extracted invoices against BigQuery data earlier in the process so teams can identify duplicates and mismatches sooner, reducing the number of invoices that require manual review.</span></p>
</li>
<li style="vertical-align: baseline;">
<p><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">Stronger auditability</strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">: Maintaining a clear trail of data from source to operational output.</span></p>
</li>
<li style="vertical-align: baseline;">
<p><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">Reduced data movement</strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">: Keeping data resident in BigQuery and leverage its built-in capabilities for enhanced security, governance, and control.</span></p>
</li>
</ul>

<div class="block-paragraph_advanced"><p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">At a high level, invoice PDFs are staged in Cloud Storage, processed through a Live Query workflow in the browser, reconciled against governed invoice history already resident in BigQuery, and written back as operational outputs for finance teams. The key architectural advantage of this pattern is that workflow authoring happens in the browser, while transformation and reconciliation logic are executed through platform services against the governed data environment.</span></p>
<p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">The </span><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">Live Query </strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">architecture is designed for warehouse-scale processing and governed execution.</span><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;"> </strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">It uses secure SQL pushdown to execute transformation and reconciliation logic directly in </span><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">BigQuery</strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">, and integrates with </span><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">Gemini </strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">for advanced AI capabilities such as document extraction, classification, and summarization.</span></p>
<ol>
<li style="vertical-align: baseline;">
<p><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">Live Query (Browser):</strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;"> Users author workflows visually in the browser, configuring document extraction, classification, cleansing, validation, and routing logic.</span></p>
</li>
<li style="vertical-align: baseline;">
<p><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">Alteryx One Platform Services: </strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">The </span><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">Transformation Service</strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;"> coordinates request handling, execution planning, and result retrieval between the browser workflow and the customer data environment. The services also add traceability and observability across workflow execution, strengthening both operational monitoring and auditability.</span></p>
</li>
<li style="vertical-align: baseline;">
<p><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">Customer Google Cloud</strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">: Invoice PDFs are staged in </span><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">Google Cloud Storage (GCS)</strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">, historical invoice data remains governed in </span><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">BigQuery</strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">, AI-backed extraction runs against that environment, and the workflow outputs are written back into BigQuery.</span></p>
</li>
</ol>
<p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">In this pattern, the browser is the authoring layer, </span><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">Alteryx One Platform Services</strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;"> are the coordination layer, and</span><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;"> Google Cloud</strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;"> is the governed data and execution layer. It allows workflow logic to be authored visually in </span><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">Live Query</strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;"> while execution remains aligned with governed data and warehouse-scale processing in </span><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">BigQuery</strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">.</span></p>

<div class="block-paragraph_advanced"><p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">Let’s take a closer look at how the Live Query workflow handles unstructured PDFs, using an invoice as the example.</span></p>
<p><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">Ingest invoice PDFs<br /></strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">The workflow begins by pointing to a directory input in Google Cloud Storage, where the raw PDF files are stored. In practice, this step does more than just identify a folder: it enumerates the PDF locations and returns file-level metadata — such as absolute file paths, size, and created / updated timestamps — so the workflow can reliably pass the right document set into downstream extraction steps.</span></p>
<p><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">Extract key fields <br /></strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">Using Alteryx’s Document Extract tool, the workflow processes PDF invoices stored in Google Cloud Storage and extracts structured or semi-structured results directly in BigQuery. The extraction step uses the user-selected model, either Document AI or Gemini AI, depending on the workflow configuration. Under the hood, the tool builds a temporary external object table from the input document URIs and executes the appropriate function — ML.PROCESS_DOCUMENT for Document AI model or AI.GENERATE for Gemini AI model. For this workflow, the Gemini path is used, with AI.GENERATE returning the requested invoice fields into the downstream Live Query workflow. </span></p>
<p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">During interactive design, Live Query limits processing to a small subset of document URIs for preview, while runtime execution processes the full set of invoice PDFs.</span></p></div>
<div class="block-code"><dl>
    <dt>code_block</dt>
    <dd>&lt;ListValue: [StructValue([(&#x27;code&#x27;, &quot;-- illustrative pattern only\r\nSELECT *\r\nFROM AI.GENERATE(\r\n  MODEL project.dataset.gemini_model,\r\n  (\r\n    SELECT\r\n      uri,\r\n      &#x27;Extract invoice_number, invoice_amount, vendor_name, net_amount, tax_amount, and line_items.&#x27; AS prompt\r\n    FROM project.dataset.temp_invoice_object_table\r\n  )\r\n);&quot;), (&#x27;language&#x27;, &#x27;&#x27;), (&#x27;caption&#x27;, &lt;wagtail.rich_text.RichText object at 0x7f5a89ba8ed0&gt;)])]&gt;</dd>
</dl></div>
<div class="block-paragraph_advanced"><p><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">Classify extracted content<br /></strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">After extraction, the workflow uses Alteryx’s Classify tool to apply zero-shot classification to the extracted invoice text. In this use case, line-item content is labeled into predefined business categories — such as industrial or office supplies — using AI.CLASSIFY in BigQuery, which returns a new predicted-label column into the downstream workflow. This business context makes the extracted data more useful for downstream reconciliation, exception handling, and reporting.</span></p></div>

</dl></div>
<div class="block-paragraph_advanced"><p><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">Normalize data<br /></strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">The extracted invoice numbers are cleansed and standardized so that minor formatting differences — such as whitespace or inconsistent formatting — do not hide potential duplicates during reconciliation.</span></p>
<p><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">Compare against history<br /></strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">The workflow joins the normalized current invoice records against historical invoice data already resident in BigQuery. This reconciliation step identifies records with matching invoice numbers already present in history and flags them as potential duplicates.</span></p>
<p><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">Flag exceptions<br /></strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">A formula step then applies business-rule logic to the remaining records. Those rules can vary by workflow and may include checks for amount mismatches, missing required fields, invalid totals, or other finance-specific exceptions. In this example, one rule checks whether </span><span style="font-style: italic; vertical-align: baseline;">invoice_amount</span><span style="vertical-align: baseline;"> matches </span><span style="font-style: italic; vertical-align: baseline;">net_amount + tax_amount</span><span style="vertical-align: baseline;"> and flags mismatches for review.</span></p>
<p><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">Produce operational outputs<br /></strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">The workflow produces two operational outputs: one for invoices matched to historical records and flagged as potential duplicates, and another for unmatched current invoices that have been validated, labeled for exceptions where needed, and prepared for unpaid-invoice processing.</span></p>
<p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">The result is not just extracted data, but governed operational outputs that finance teams can review and act on immediately.</span></p>
